# Schema Reference

Use this reference when checking the formal Book-to-Comic project contract. The single official episode file is `story/episode-001.json`; legacy `episodes/EP01/episode.json` may only be read by `migrate-legacy-project`.

## Official Project Layout

Formal projects contain `project.json`, `manifest.json`, `state.json`, `source/`, `planning/`, `story/episode-001.json`, `prompts/`, `text-prompts/`, `assets/`, `qa/`, `review/`, `final/`, and `tmp/`.

`story/episode-001.json` must not use root-level `panels[]` for final production. Formal panels live under `scenes[].panels[]`.

## EpisodePackage 3.0

Required top-level fields include `schema_version`, `planning_status`, `episode_id`, `book_title`, `episode_title`, `primary_thesis`, `source_refs`, `style_id`, `episode_format`, `adaptation_strategy`, `narrative_spine`, `concept_units`, `rhythm_plan`, `story_ir`, and `scenes`.

`episode_format` controls rhythm bounds:

- `short_hook`: 4-6 total panels, 2-3 scenes.
- `standard`: 6-10 total panels, 3-5 scenes.
- `deep_scene`: 10-16 total panels, 3-6 scenes.

`adaptation_strategy` must include `soul_conflict`, `chosen_form`, `why_fits`, `avoided_cliches`, and `novelty_hook`. `narrative_spine` must include `protagonist`, `foil`, `spine_event`, `opening_struggle`, and `this_episode_change`.

## Scene

Each scene is a causal event, not a point card. Required fields include `scene_id`, `location`, `kind`, `story_function`, `cause_from_previous`, `character_goal`, `obstacle`, `attempt`, `turn`, `decision`, `consequence`, `stakes_before`, `stakes_after`, `new_information`, `irreversible_change`, `unremovable_reason`, `concept_carried`, `source_refs`, `emotion_arc`, and `panels`.

The first scene must explain the `narrative_spine.spine_event.trigger`. Later scenes must be caused by the previous scene's consequence. `stakes_after` must not be identical to `stakes_before` unless another concrete change is present. `unremovable_reason` must name the dependency that breaks if the scene is removed.

## ConceptUnit And Grounding

`concept_units[]` maps abstract ideas into traceable claims. Each item needs `concept_id`, `label`, `claim`, `mechanism`, `evidence`, `boundary_conditions`, `counterexample`, `implication`, `source_refs`, and `factuality`.

Panels and scenes use `semantic_anchor_ids` such as `CU01.claim` or `CU02.mechanism`. Key claims cannot be supported only by invented bridges.

Formal panels use `grounding`, not the old `life_scene` requirement. `grounding.type` may be `contemporary_life`, `historical_reconstruction`, `institutional_process`, `source_world`, `material_detail`, `analogy`, `counterexample`, or `diagrammatic`. History, politics, and institutional books should not be forced into mostly contemporary-life scenes.

## StoryIR

`story_ir` includes `source_chunks`, `scene_beats`, and `panel_scripts`. `build-story-ir` only normalizes explicit relationships and computes deterministic metrics. It must not infer `source_refs` by scene order, cycle `panel_role`, or slice `action_verb` from prose.

Missing `source_refs`, `panel_role`, or `action_verb` is a planning error.

## Panel

Each formal panel includes `panel_id`, `source_refs`, `semantic_anchor_ids`, `grounding`, `panel_role`, `reading_order`, `location` or `setting`, `characters_present`, `action`, `action_verb`, `emotion`, `performance`, `shot_size`, `continuity_from_prev`, `transition_to_next`, `beat_duration`, `visual_density`, `text_density`, `visual_weight`, `pause_after`, `reveal_priority`, `layout_hint`, `text_layers`, `text_variants`, `text_budgets`, `balloon_budget`, `lettering_layout`, `image_prompt`, and status or asset fields.

`text_variants.long_form` is for long-image narrative reading AND is what delivered cards render (cards and the long image share it). `text_variants.card_form` survives only as an internal compact variant for legacy gates and may have at most three paragraphs. They must preserve the same semantic anchors and cannot be generated by silent slicing.

## CharacterBible

Major characters need narrative function, appeal strategy, shape language, face geometry, body design, wardrobe materials, performance habits, expression keyframes, inner engine, and relationship power changes. Characters that differ only by color fail validation.

## EnvironmentBible

Each environment needs function in the story, era/geography, action paths, spatial anchors, materials, lighting, recurring props, and supported grounding modes. The validator checks specificity and rejects generic study-room/world templates.

## Visual And Text Contracts

`planning/visual_identity.json` locks book-specific adaptation form, cast model, character system, environment system, motifs, palette/lighting, camera/composition, and forbidden carry-over. `planning/visual_contract.json` stores `visual_identity_sha256`, character/environment/prop state, and reference pack panel locks.

`planning/narrative_identity.json` locks voice core, speaker registers, forbidden phrases, and text policy. Text QA writes `qa/text-richness-report.json`; text distinctiveness writes `qa/text-distinctiveness-report.json`.

## Reports

Formal gates write `qa/source-claim-report.json`, `qa/story-gate-report.json`, `qa/visual-identity-report.json`, `qa/distinctiveness-report.json`, `qa/visual-contract-report.json`, `qa/text-richness-report.json`, `qa/text-distinctiveness-report.json`, `qa/lettering-layout-report.json`, and `qa/rhythm-layout-report.json`.
